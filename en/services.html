01 / BUSINESS LINES

Four specialized divisions across the energy value chain.

Power generation infrastructure, upstream-to-downstream oil and gas, logistics supply chain for refined products trading, and commercial agency. In every mandate we structure the operation, manage contracts and documentation, and supervise licensed third-party operators.

BUSINESS LINE 01

Power Generation

Technical structuring, equipment procurement and project management for utility-scale and industrial power generation. We work with project owners, developers and institutional investors across thermal and gas-fired plants, photovoltaic solar parks, wind farms and battery storage systems, coordinating OEM equipment selection, fuel supply logistics and EPC contracting through to commissioning.

  • Thermal, simple-cycle and combined-cycle gas turbine plants
  • Utility-scale photovoltaic solar parks and onshore/offshore wind complexes
  • New technologies: battery energy storage (BESS), green hydrogen and hybrid microgrids
  • Feasibility structuring, OEM procurement and EPC contract oversight

Executing entities: Zenith Energy Group LLC

Thermal & Gas-Fired

Simple-cycle and combined-cycle gas turbine plants (OCGT / CCGT) and reciprocating engine power stations: fuel supply logistics, turbine and generator procurement, balance of plant and EPC contract management.

Photovoltaic Solar

Utility-scale and industrial ground-mounted photovoltaic plants: site assessment, Tier-1 module and central inverter procurement, substation equipment and grid interconnection management from feasibility to commercial operation.

Onshore & Offshore Wind

Onshore and offshore wind power developments: wind resource campaigns, turbine and tower supply agreements, heavy-lift maritime and overland transport logistics, and civil-electrical balance of plant oversight.

BUSINESS LINE 02

Oil & Gas

Integrated technical procurement, field equipment supply and midstream-downstream coordination across the hydrocarbon value chain. From wellhead tubulars, valves and surface production systems in upstream campaigns to third-party terminal storage, marine transport and refined product supply for industrial off-takers and distributors.

  • Upstream: API tubulars, wellheads, process valves, artificial lift and field services
  • Midstream: storage capacity at third-party tank terminals, pipeline and maritime transport
  • Downstream: refined product supply and engineered equipment for refineries and process plants

Executing entities: Zenith Energy Group LLC; RINCONCD67 Consulting & Supply LLC

Upstream

Exploration and production support. Procurement of API casing and tubing (OCTG), wellhead assemblies, high-pressure gate and ball valves, artificial lift systems and specialized field services for drilling and workover campaigns.

Midstream

Bulk storage and transportation. Contracting of tank capacity at independent maritime terminals, pipeline scheduling and coastal or international tanker transport, always executed through licensed third-party operators.

Downstream

Refining, process equipment and distribution. Supply of refined petroleum products for wholesale distributors and industrial consumers, alongside high-pressure valves, piping and process equipment for refineries and petrochemical plants.

BUSINESS LINE 03

Logistics Supply Chain for Refined Products Trading

End-to-end operational execution and documentary coordination for bulk cargoes of diesel, gasoline, jet fuel, fuel oil and marine bunkers. We structure and supervise the physical supply chain between load port and discharge terminal: storage and blending nominations at third-party terminals, vessel chartering and land haulage with licensed carriers, independent quantity and quality inspection, and shipping documentation control.

  • Middle distillates (ULSD / gasoil), motor gasoline, Jet A-1, fuel oil and marine bunkers
  • Tank storage, custom blending and throughput coordination at third-party terminals
  • Maritime chartering and overland tank-truck transport with licensed carriers
  • Independent quantity and quality custody-transfer inspection at load and discharge ports
  • Documentary execution, laytime tracking and delivery schedule control

Executing entities: Zenith Energy Group LLC

BUSINESS LINE 04

Agency and Consulting

Contractual commercial representation and strategic advisory across the Americas, Europe and international energy corridors. Industrial manufacturers, refined product sellers, service station franchise brands and turnkey EPC contractors appoint us to represent their commercial interests before qualified buyers and institutional investors, managing technical qualification, commercial negotiation and contract closing.

  • Commercial representation of industrial OEM manufacturers and commodity sellers under mandate
  • Structuring and representation of retail fuel station franchises and turnkey EPC contractors
  • Sectorial consulting, market entry analysis and supply chain feasibility studies
  • Technical qualification, commercial structuring and contract follow-through

Executing entities: RINCONCD67 Consulting & Supply LLC; Zenith Energy Group LLC

Representation of Manufacturers & Sellers

Industrial equipment manufacturers and bulk fuel sellers appoint us under commercial agency mandates to position their lines before qualified buyers, structure technical-commercial offers and coordinate contract execution.

Sectorial & Technical Consulting

Advisory on market entry, supply chain structuring, technical procurement and project feasibility for energy, oilfield and infrastructure companies operating across the Americas and Europe.

Franchises & Turnkey EPC Projects

Commercial representation of downstream fuel station franchise brands and turnkey industrial EPC contractors before qualified regional investors, developers and infrastructure operators.

ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORKS

Two ways to structure an operation

01 / Modular Scope Execution

Targeted management of a specific component required by the client: equipment procurement, terminal storage, maritime chartering, independent inspection or a defined engineering phase.

02 / Integrated Turnkey Mandate

Comprehensive project direction or end-to-end supply chain coordination under a master services agreement, centralizing technical oversight, third-party contracting and documentary control.

OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

How an engagement progresses

  1. Technical InquiryThe client outlines the project scope, equipment specifications or product volumes, origin and destination corridors, and target schedule.
  2. Feasibility & DesignWe evaluate technical specifications, logistics routes, terminal availability and OEM manufacturer options to design the execution structure.
  3. Commercial ProposalWe issue a written commercial and technical scope detailing responsibilities, delivery terms, schedule and fee or price structure.
  4. Supervised ExecutionWe contract the required operators and suppliers, coordinate independent inspection, track milestones and close the operation.

Each group company contracts in its own legal name. Depending on the nature of the mandate, Zenith Energy Group LLC may act as commercial agent on behalf of the client or as principal in its own name, as expressly agreed in the signed contract.

Zenith Energy Group LLC does not own or operate power plants, storage terminals, ocean vessels or transport fleets. Physical operations are performed by licensed third parties contracted case by case.
